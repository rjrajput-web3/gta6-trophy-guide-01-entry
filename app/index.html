⚠ Missable first: Positive Mental Attitude

The list has exactly one missable trophy. Meditate at all 18 Meditation Spots — but one meditation is not at a marked spot. In Chapter 1, after an early mission, Lucia mentions meditation in the kitchen at Jason’s house one morning and heads to the balcony. Follow her and accept the prompt. The Progress counter rises by 1, yet the balcony is never marked as a Meditation Spot, and the moment cannot be repeated after Chapter 1. Do it the moment it is offered and the platinum stays safe. Nothing else in the list can be missed.

The platinum roadmap — four stages, in order

One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep a save at the start and end of every chapter (10 slots; rotate the other 5 in free roam). Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off your platinum save. Tick trophies off here or in the guide; progress is shared and saved.

About this list: 55 trophies — 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze — with names, descriptions and grades as PlayStation publishes them on the official trophy list. All 55 are single-player; there are no online trophies and one playthrough is enough. Guides are written for this page and cite their sources beside each trophy (the official list, PowerPyx, GamesRadar+). Six trophies — Fair Game, In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin — still have unconfirmed triggers in the published lists; they are flagged as unconfirmed everywhere in this guide and no trigger is invented for them. Difficulty and time figures are community estimates, not official data.

Progress is stored only in your browser (localStorage) and can be exported or imported as a file. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.
