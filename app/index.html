        Sources & how this guide was built

        Every trophy's name, description and grade is the official PlayStation list as published on Exophase and PSNProfiles, who agree on all 55. Each guide is written in our own words from the official description, and names where its facts come from. A guide that cites no source earns no credit here, so every trophy below carries its sources.

          Method notes

          • Counts (42 Shooting Range challenges, 250 stunts, 100 WAiNK locations and the rest) come from the official descriptions and the progress counts PSN tracks.
          • Where a location is not pinned down by the official description, the guide says so instead of inventing one.
          • No Rockstar logo, artwork or game screenshot is used anywhere in this guide. The look is our own.
          • The guide works fully offline: every file it loads sits under app/, and it makes no network request at run time.
