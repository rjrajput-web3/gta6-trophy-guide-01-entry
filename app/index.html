    The road to the platinum

    Built from the official descriptions: five stages, in order. Finish one stage before the next where the guide says so — the missable trophies live in stages 1 and 2.

      Missable trophies — do these before the finale

      Eight trophies need a person, a choice or a moment that the story can take away. Everything else in the list is safe to clean up after the credits, or replayable from the pause menu.
