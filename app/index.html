The platinum roadmap — three phases

  1. Phase 1 — Story & side missions, 60–80 hours. Play through the story and pick up optional missions as they appear. Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app and study animals through your binoculars. Everything before the ending already counts toward the 100% checklist.
  2. Phase 2 — The 100% checklist & Challenges, about 100 hours. After the ending, Progress > Stats gains a 100% Completion entry. It is closer to 60% true completion: most side quests are not required, and collectibles only partly. You do need every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest. Only 70 of the 80 Challenges are needed, so skip the nastiest ones.
  3. Phase 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup, about 10 hours. The checklist does not ask for these quests, so they are usually what is left afterwards, together with any stray trophies that did not pop on their own.

Save smart: there is no mission replay and no New Game+. Keep a separate save at the start and end of every chapter — that is 10 slots; rotate the other 5 in free roam after the ending. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off your platinum save.

⚠ The one trophy you might miss

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 Meditation Spots. After one of the early Chapter 1 missions, Lucia mentions meditation in the kitchen at Jason’s house one morning and heads out to the balcony. Follow her, accept the prompt, and she will show you how it works. The meditation counter in the Progress menu goes up by 1, yet the balcony is not marked as a Meditation Spot like the others. You cannot do it again after Chapter 1, so just do it. Nothing else you can miss affects trophies.
