Do this first — the one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida) is the only trophy the published guides flag as possibly missable. The first meditation is Lucia on the balcony at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, and it is reported to disappear once that chapter ends. Whether it counts toward the 18 is unconfirmed, so do it in Chapter 1 and let the final zen animation play. Everything else can be cleaned up in free roam after the story.

The road to the Platinum in seven stages

Roughly 200–300 hours in total, most of it the road to 100% completion. Play the story first, clean up after it.

  1. Stage 1 — Before you start. Cheats off: using a cheat disables trophies and saving for the session. Turn the spoiler setting on if you want story trophies hidden. In Chapter 1, do the balcony meditation at Jason’s house.
  2. Stage 2 — Play the story. Enjoy the campaign at your own pace. Twelve story trophies pop as the chapters and key missions finish, ending with the Gold for the final mission. Free roam stays open after the credits.
  3. Stage 3 — Stranger missions. Eight stranger strands run through the game. Take each one the moment it appears: at least one guide reports that some stranger chains are tied to story progress. Their finales carry their own trophies.
  4. Stage 4 — Try everything once. Sample all nine activity types for Master of None, then work the shooting ranges for gold in all 42 challenges, win the 14 drone races and 20 different races, and bowl a frame or two.
  5. Stage 5 — Collections. Cards (3 sets for the trophy, 15 for 100%), 100 WAiNK scans, 100 vehicles discovered, 18 meditation spots. Scan everything you pass; it all counts twice.
  6. Stage 6 — Crime and money. Five bank vaults, an armored truck, three shops back-to-back with your partner, a jewelry store without being seen. Launder $250,000 of Marked Bills, spend $100,000 in shops, buy all eight garages ($160,000) and register ten vehicles.
  7. Stage 7 — The 100% checklist. The checklist appears under Progress after the story. Finish it for A Long Road: 250 of the 397 stunts, 50 of the 80 challenges (70 for 100%), and the rest. The Platinum follows the moment the last trophy pops.

    Facts worth knowing

    • 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze. The same list is the 55 achievements on Xbox Series X|S, worth 1,000 Gamerscore.
    • No online trophies. Everything is single-player and offline. One playthrough is enough, and there are no mission gold medals and no mission replay.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving for the session, so keep them off for the whole run.
    • 28 of the 55 stay hidden on PlayStation until earned, which is why the spoiler setting hides story trophies here until you tick them.
    • Five hidden trophies are still unsolved in the published guides (In the Beginning..., Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin). Their entries below say so honestly instead of guessing.
    • List status: Rockstar has not published an official list. Names, descriptions and grades are the list reported on 18 November 2026 and cross-checked across five guides; see Sources below.
