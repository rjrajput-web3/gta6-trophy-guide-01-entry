The road to the platinum

Six stages, in the order to play them. Stages 1 to 3 follow the story, so the missable trophies are cleared while their moments are still open. Stages 4 and 5 are the free-roam grind, and stage 6 is the final sweep to 100%.

Missable trophies

These trophies are tied to one story moment: a single mission, a partner job, or a one-time place. Do each one on your first pass, while its moment is open. Everything else in the list can be earned in free roam at any time.

Where the facts come from

The trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list, as published on the trophy tracking sites below. Every guide on this site is written in our own words for this guide; the sources are cited beside each trophy so you can check any fact.

  • Exophase trophy list — official trophy names, descriptions and grades for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5.
  • PSNProfiles trophy list — the same official list, cross-checked name for name against Exophase.
  • IGN GTA 6 wiki — activity and mission guidance: shooting range, races, drone races, fishing and side activities.
  • GTA Wiki — collectibles, exploration and Leonida locations: meditation spots, cards, vehicles and areas.
  • Rockstar Games official GTA VI site — the game's own announcements and world details.

A source is cited, never copied. If a guide here ever disagrees with the official list, the official list wins.
