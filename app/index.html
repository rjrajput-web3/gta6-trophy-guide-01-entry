Roadmap to the Platinum

  1. Phase 1 — Story & side missions 60–80 hours

    Play the story at your leisure and pick up optional missions as they appear: purple icons, plus black icons with pink letters. Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app and study animals through your binoculars — 100% needs 200 of 277 vehicle models and 100 of 255 animals. Everything you do before the ending already counts toward the checklist.

  2. Phase 2 — The 100% checklist & Challenges about 100 hours

    After the ending, Progress > Stats gains a 100% Completion entry. You need every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest, plus 70 of the 80 Challenges and 15 of the 18 card sets. Most other trophies pop along the way — this is the long middle of the run.

  3. Phase 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup about 10 hours

    The checklist does not ask for the stranger questlines, so they are usually what is left afterwards, together with any stray trophies that did not pop on their own. Finish the five side-character finals, then sweep the open-world trophies.

No mission replay exists, so keep a separate manual save at the start and end of every chapter (15 manual slots). Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off your Platinum save.

Missable trophies

Only one trophy can possibly be missed. Everything else is still earnable in free roam after the ending.

Bronze

Positive Mental Attitude

Meditate at every spot across Leonida.

After an early Chapter 1 mission, Lucia mentions meditation in the kitchen at Jason’s house and heads out to the balcony. Follow her, accept the prompt, and meditate there. The Progress counter goes up by 1, yet the balcony is not marked as a Meditation Spot like the other 18 — and you cannot do it again after Chapter 1. PowerPyx has not confirmed whether the trophy needs it, so just do it.
